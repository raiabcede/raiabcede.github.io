Privacy Notice

Last updated: November 19, 2026. This notice explains how your information is collected when you access the resume and documents page at abcede.dev/dict.

1. Why We Ask for Your Email

This site contains private personal documents and credentials. To protect these documents from unauthorized access, visitors are required to provide their email address before viewing the content.

Your email is collected strictly to identify who has visited the page. This allows the site owner, Ryan Abcede, to maintain a log of authorized viewers and detect any unauthorized access.

2. What Information Is Collected

When you submit the access form, the following information is recorded:

  • Email address — as entered in the access form.
  • IP address — your device's public IP address at the time of access.
  • Visit timestamp — the date and time of your visit (Philippine Standard Time).
  • Page URL — the specific page accessed.

3. How This Information Is Used

The collected information is used solely for the following purposes:

  • To verify that access was granted only to invited or authorized individuals.
  • To identify and investigate any unauthorized or suspicious access.
  • To maintain an access log for the site owner's personal records.

Your information will not be used for marketing, advertising, or any purpose beyond access monitoring.

4. Data Storage and Sharing

Submitted data is processed through Formspree and delivered directly to the site owner's email inbox. It is not stored in a public database and will not be sold, rented, or shared with any third party.

5. Your Rights

You may request the deletion of your access record by contacting the site owner directly. Access to this site is voluntary — if you do not wish to provide your email, do not proceed past the verification step.

6. Contact

For any questions about this privacy notice, contact rai@abcede.dev.
